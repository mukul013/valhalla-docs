# Unlock Robinhood beta

1. Sign in to the website.
2. Open **Account** in the left rail. The page is titled **Profile**.
3. Find the **Beta features** card.
4. Type the code in **Beta passcode** and click **Unlock**. An **Unlocked** badge appears.
5. Open **Robinhood LP** in the left rail.

The passcode opens every beta feature and works on every device you sign in on. Unlock it on the website.

Before you unlock, the page says "Robinhood LP Copy is in beta" and offers **Open Profile > Beta**. It also notes that Meteora LP on Solana is open to everyone in the meantime.
