# Referrals and affiliates

## How it works

- Share your referral code with friends.
- When they start using the bot, they are linked to you automatically.
- You earn **20% of the trading fees they pay to Valhalla**, sent to your wallet in SOL.
- Fees split automatically. No manual payouts.

## Get your code

1. Open **Referrals** in the left rail.
2. Copy **Your invite link**.
3. Share it. A new user can sign up through your link, or paste your code into the optional **Referral code** box when they run `/valhalla start`.
