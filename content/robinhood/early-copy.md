Early copy is opt-in per follow. Valhalla reads the sequencer feed and starts your copy before the target's transaction is confirmed. This gets you a price closer to the target's.

## Turn it on

1. Open the follow.
2. Switch on **Early copy**.
3. Save.

Exits use one transaction for v3 and one batch for v4, so removals land together.
