---
title: Withdraw
description: Move ETH from your Robinhood wallet to another address.
beta: true
---

Withdrawal moves native ETH on Robinhood Chain. It is not a bridge.

1. Open **Robinhood > Wallets > Withdraw**, or use the **Withdrawal** button in `/valhalla robinhood`.
2. Paste the destination address on Robinhood Chain.
3. Enter the amount.
4. Approve. Every withdrawal needs your approval.

::shot[Withdraw page | /dashboard/robinhood/wallets/withdraw with address and amount | The approval button | robinhood-withdraw.png]

:::warn
Check the address twice. Sending to an address on another chain cannot be recovered.
:::
