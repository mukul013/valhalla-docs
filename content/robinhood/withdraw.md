title: Withdraw
description: Send ETH or USDG from your Robinhood wallet to another address.
beta: true
---

Withdrawal sends ETH or USDG on Robinhood Chain. It is not a bridge.

1. Open **Robinhood LP > Wallets** and click **Withdraw** (in the Funding card). From Discord, use **Withdraw on website** on the Wallet screen.
2. Choose **ETH** or **USDG**.
3. Paste the destination address on Robinhood Chain and enter the **Amount**. The available balance is shown.
4. Click **Review withdrawal**. The review shows the amount, estimated network fee, destination, source wallet and network.
5. Click **Approve**.

Withdrawals in progress are listed with **Check status**. If signing permission is missing, the page shows **Signing permission needed**.

::shot[Withdraw page | /dashboard/robinhood/wallets/withdraw with the review step | The asset toggle, Amount and the Approve button | robinhood-withdraw.png]

:::warn
Check the address twice. Funds sent to an address on another chain cannot be recovered.
:::
