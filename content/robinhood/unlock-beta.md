---
title: Unlock Robinhood beta
description: Enter the passcode on the website to open Robinhood Chain.
beta: true
---

1. Sign in to the website.
2. Open **Profile**, then **Beta**.
3. Enter the passcode.
4. Open **Robinhood** in the left rail.

The passcode opens all beta features. Discord cannot unlock beta. If you are locked out you see "Robinhood LP Copy is in beta. Unlock it in Profile > Beta."
