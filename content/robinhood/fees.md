---
title: Robinhood fees
description: What Valhalla charges on Robinhood Chain.
beta: true
---

- **Performance fee:** charged on the LP fees you earn, in the same transaction as a close, removal, claim or v4 increase. It shows as a fee line in Activity. The rate is shown there too.
- **Gas:** paid in ETH.
- **Referrals:** see [Referrals](../account/referrals.md).

There is no opening fee shown for Robinhood Chain.
