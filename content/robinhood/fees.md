---
title: Robinhood fees
description: What Valhalla charges on Robinhood Chain.
beta: true
---

- **Performance fee:** taken from the LP fees you earn, in the same transaction as a close, removal, claim or v4 increase. It shows as a fee line in Activity.
- **Gas:** always paid in ETH. USDG-base accounts get an automatic gas refill from small USDG swaps.
- **Referrals:** see [Referrals](../account/referrals.md).

:::note
The performance fee rate is shown in Activity once it is active for your account.
:::
