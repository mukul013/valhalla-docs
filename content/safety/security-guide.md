---
title: Security Guide
description: Keep your wallet and Discord account safe.
---

1. **Set a PIN when you export keys.** Run `/valhalla export_keys` after start. A PIN means a stolen Discord login alone cannot reach your funds. A forgotten PIN can be reset after a waiting period.
2. **Keep exported keys offline.** Write them down and store them somewhere physical. Never in cloud notes or messages.
3. **Keep little in the wallet.** Only what you can afford to lose. Withdraw profits often.
4. **Use risk controls.** SOL Only, stop-loss, single pool per token, minimum age, market cap and Jupiter score filters, and max open positions.
5. **Check your ratio.** A wrong ratio can lose more than the wallet you copy. Use `/ratio-help`.
6. **Secure Discord.** Turn on two-factor on Discord and on its email. Do not share trade screenshots publicly. Do not click links or run commands from strangers.
7. **Take profits out.** Move SOL or USDC to a hardware wallet or trusted exchange.

## Checklist

- [ ] PIN set on export
- [ ] Keys stored offline
- [ ] Ratio checked
- [ ] Discord 2FA on
- [ ] Profits withdrawn regularly

:::note
For Robinhood Chain, you also give a signing authorization. Review what it permits before you approve.
:::
