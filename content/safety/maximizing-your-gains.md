## Manage risk

- Require tokens at least a few hours old (ideally 8), market cap above $800k (ideally above $2M) and Jupiter score above 55.
- Match your ratio to the wallet's trade sizes.
- Set a stop-loss and a take-profit.
- Use SOL Only and one pool per token.
- If needed, cap new positions to one every six hours.

## Pick wallets well

Skip wallets that trade mostly tokens, small caps under $2M, or have too many followers. Follow one or two wallets.

## Check your ratio

If you underperform the wallet, your ratio may be off. A wallet can lose big on a trade that was a small part of its capital. If you cap your size, you absorb its losses but miss its big wins.

## Optimize fees

Set priority fees to Medium or Low and cap the Jito fee near 0.00005 to 0.0001 SOL.

## Green PnL, red wallet

1. The PnL image shows a gain such as 0.46 SOL, but your balance drops.
2. Check the 1-minute chart at the close time. In one example the token fell 13.25% in that minute, so the exit came after the drop.
3. Review the position's exit value. Price movement during the swap and Meteora fees reduce what you actually receive.
4. On Solscan, add the swap proceeds and the position proceeds and compare to your deposit. In the example, 16.75 SOL plus 12.35 SOL is 29.1 SOL against a 30 SOL deposit, a 0.9 SOL loss.
