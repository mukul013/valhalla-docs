## Activity

Open **Meteora LP > Activity**. Three views:

- **Activity** (default): copies that went through.
- **Skipped:** trades a rule blocked, each with the reason. Use **Send message again** to resend a notice.
- **Results:** closed positions and their PnL.

Skipped is the first place to look when you expected a copy and did not get one.

::shot[Activity skipped | /dashboard/meteora/activity/skipped with several reasons | The reason column | meteora-activity-skipped.png]

## Positions

The overview lists open positions, each with **Claim fees** and close actions. Click one for its detail page, which has the same buttons.

PnL is position value plus unclaimed fees plus claimed fees minus cost. Position rent is never counted as profit.

::shot[Position detail | An open position detail page | Value, fees, PnL, Claim fees and Close | meteora-position-detail.png]

## Rebalancing warnings

If a target keeps rebalancing the same position, you get a warning once per episode, in the app and on Discord, with a button to turn on [Smart Token Retention](smart-token-retention.md).
