## Activity

Open **Meteora > Activity**. It has three views:

- **Executed:** copies that went through.
- **Results:** closed positions and their PnL.
- **Skipped:** trades a rule blocked, each with the reason.

Skipped is the first place to look when you expected a copy and did not get one.

::shot[Activity skipped | /dashboard/meteora/activity/skipped with several reasons | The reason column | meteora-activity-skipped.png]

## Positions

The overview lists open positions. Click one for detail.

PnL is `position value + unclaimed fees + claimed fees - cost`. Position rent is never counted as profit.

::shot[Position detail | /dashboard/meteora/positions/<id> for an open position | Value, fees and PnL | meteora-position-detail.png]

## Look up any wallet

Use **Look up** to paste a wallet and see its profit and loss, number of positions, frequency and win rate. See [Wallet research](../help/wallet-research.md).
