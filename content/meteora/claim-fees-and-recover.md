---

## Claim fees

1. Open **Meteora > Overview**.
2. Find the position and click **Claim fees**.
3. Confirm.

Claims under $2 are not processed. The claim fee is shown on the confirmation. See [Fees](fees.md).

## Reclaim rent

Empty and dust token accounts lock a little SOL each.

1. Open **Meteora > Wallets**, then **Reclaim**.
2. Review what can be recovered: reclaimable rent and Pump cashback.
3. Run the recovery. It is recorded in your activity.

Every few weeks you can also use a burn tool such as Burnlify to burn old tokens and close empty accounts.
