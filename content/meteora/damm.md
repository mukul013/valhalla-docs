:::beta
DAMM copying is a beta feature. Unlock in **Profile > Beta**. See [Beta features](../account/beta-features.md).
:::

DAMM is Meteora's dynamic AMM. Valhalla can copy those pools as well as DLMM.

:::warn
DAMM pools are mostly new tokens, and most of them rug. Use small sizes.
:::

## Turn on DAMM for a follow

1. Open the follow editor.
2. Switch on **Copy DAMM**.
3. Set the **DAMM ratio** and **DAMM amount**.
4. Save.

::shot[DAMM toggles | Follow editor with Copy DAMM, DAMM ratio and DAMM amount | The three DAMM fields | meteora-damm.png]

Entry mode does not apply to DAMM. DAMM positions show no live valuation on the overview.
