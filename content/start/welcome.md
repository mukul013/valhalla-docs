Valhalla watches the wallets you follow. When one of them opens, adds to or closes a liquidity position, Valhalla does the same from your wallet, scaled to the size you set. You choose who to follow, how much to risk, and which tokens to skip.

## Choose your LP

Each LP has its own wallet, settings and guide. Your account is shared, so you can use more than one.

## Use it from the website or from Discord

The website is the full interface. Discord covers core management and sends you trade updates by direct message. Some things work only on the website, such as unlocking beta features and Robinhood token risk filters.
