One passcode opens every beta feature. You enter it on the website. You cannot enter it in Discord.

## Unlock

1. Sign in to the website.
2. Open **Profile**, then the **Beta** tab.
3. Enter the passcode and confirm.
4. The locked pages open. Features you have not unlocked show "is in beta" with a link to this tab.

## What is in beta

| Feature | What it does | Guide |
|---|---|---|
| Robinhood LP Copy | Copy LP positions on Robinhood Chain | [Robinhood Chain](../robinhood/overview.md) |
| Multi-wallet | Copy from more than one Solana wallet | [Wallets and multi-wallet](wallets-and-multi-wallet.md) |
| Presets | Follow a curated group of target wallets | [Presets](../meteora/presets.md) |
| DAMM | Copy Meteora DAMM pools, chosen per follow | [DAMM](../meteora/damm.md) |

Meteora DLMM copy trading on Solana is open to everyone.

:::note
Beta features can change. When one graduates, its Beta badge disappears from these docs and the app.
:::
