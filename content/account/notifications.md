Valhalla sends a direct message when a trade opens or closes, with a PnL image on closes.

## Turn them on

1. Authorize the bot so it can send you direct messages (the Discord authorization link in [Quick Start](../start/quick-start.md)).
2. On the website, open **Meteora > Settings** and find the **Notifications** section.
3. Choose what you want to hear about.

::shot[Notifications section | /dashboard/meteora/settings scrolled to Notifications | The toggles | settings-notifications.png]
::shot[Trade alert DM | A Discord DM showing a closed position with the PnL image | The PnL image and fee line | dm-pnl-image.png]

If DMs stop arriving, check that Discord still lets the bot message you, then reopen the link above.

Robinhood closes also produce a shareable PnL card.
