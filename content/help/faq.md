## General

### Can I use more than one LP?
Yes. Your account is shared, each LP has its own wallet and settings.

### Who can see the wallet feed?
The wallet feed is in the wallet-talk Discord channel. It is open to Decoder NFT holders, active subscribers and free 24-hour trial users.

### What is in beta?
Robinhood LP Copy, multi-wallet, Presets and DAMM. See [Beta features](../account/beta-features.md).

## Meteora

### How many positions at once?
Up to 50.

### What is not copied?
Liquidity added to an existing position (unless Infinite Add is on), claims under $2, and manually opened DLMMs, which are auto-closed.

### What does it cost?
See [Meteora fees](../meteora/fees.md).

### Why did my swap use Jupiter Ultra?
On swap errors the bot retries there. It charges 0.1% and may have higher slippage.

## Robinhood Chain

### Why was my copy skipped?
Open Activity > Skipped. Common reasons: below your minimum, a token risk filter, or the pool is not in your base currency. See [Token risk filters](../robinhood/token-risk-filters.md).

### Do I need Solana?
No. Robinhood uses its own EVM wallet.
